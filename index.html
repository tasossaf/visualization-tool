About this Tool

Welcome! This interactive dashboard brings together 40 verified international studies focused on maritime safety and cybersecurity.

Functioning as an interactive Decision Support System (DSS), it assists researchers, operators, and safety managers in evaluating navigational risks and supporting decision-making within the framework of maritime Safety Management Systems (SMS).

What does it show?

  • Where risks happen: Groundings, collisions, ice conditions, and modern cyber threats like GPS and AIS spoofing across key shipping routes.
  • How researchers solve them: You can see which analytical methods (like AI/Machine Learning, Risk Calculations, or Ship Collision Geometry) are preferred in different parts of the world.
  • Isolate Dominant Method: When checking "Highlight Dominant Method" on any region with ≥ 50% concentration, other methods are temporarily hidden and distant locations appear in preview windows.

Use the tabs at the top to explore specific regions, or use the filters on the right to isolate specific threats and technologies.
